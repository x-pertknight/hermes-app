Hermes – Privacy policy

Hermes is a personal, single-user application operated by Marco Amendola.

Data accessed

With the account owner's consent, Hermes requests read-only access to Gmail (gmail.readonly) and Google Calendar (calendar.readonly) for the owner's own Google account.

How data is used

Data is used only to show the owner summaries of his own email and calendar. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Storage

OAuth tokens and any processed data are stored only on the owner's own computer. Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
